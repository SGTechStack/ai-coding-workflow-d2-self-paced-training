Eitri — Built on Matt Pocock's Workflow

Custom AI skills that close the four enterprise gaps with compliance gates, automated validation, and self-steering loops

01

Architecture

Step 1base
/setup-matt-pocock-skills
Configures your issue tracker, triage labels, and the domain documentation that provides the core rules and vocabulary of your business. Run once per repository.
Step 2+
/dependency-orchestrator
Analyzes project requirements to map dependencies across stories, generating a PM report (duration, team sizing, critical path) and a developer wave plan (exact build sequence).
Step 3+
/arch-tests-plan & /arch-tests-gen
Analyzes your architectural style and generates build-failing tests that instantly block boundary violations, rather than waiting for code reviews.
02

Specification

Step 4base
/wayfinder
Breaks down complex problems into a clear sequence of step-by-step decision tickets, solving each unknown before moving forward.
Step 5base
/to-spec
Synthesises your technical decisions into a clear Technical Requirements Document (TRD) and publishes it.
Step 6+
/spec-compliance
Audits the TRD against strict policies like IM8 and ARC, catching violations to ensure the specification is fully compliant.
Gate 1 TRD Sign-off
03

Decomposition

Step 7base
/to-tickets
Converts the TRD into thin, vertical tracer-bullet tickets that cut through every layer of the app, mapping out the exact order they must be completed.
Step 8+
/issue-verification
Checks that every ticket covers all layers, is demoable, and unambiguous. Fails are fixed before tickets are published.
Gate 2 Ticket Sign-off

05

Closeout

Step 10base
/improve-codebase-architecture
Scans the codebase for technical debt and refactoring opportunities after the feature is built.
Step 11+
/arch-tests-gen-feature

/diagnosing-bugs
